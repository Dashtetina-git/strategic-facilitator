A company
is a system: a strategy built in isolation falls apart in implementation

What matters is understanding where things broke down, rather than deciding who’s to blame.

When everyone works separately, no one has the full picture. Everyone interprets the strategy through the lens of their own interests and motivations and covering their own back.

That’s why I speak with both management and frontline staff, especially those who usually remain silent, and bring their views to the meeting without the risk of being fired or causing conflicts to escalate.

The work runs
in two stages

  1. 1

    Research

    I talk with management and the team, and if the decision calls for it, I also speak with users and market experts. From these conversations,
    I distinguish between what has been proven and what
    is still just speculation.

  2. 2

    Workshop

    I address disagreements thoughtfully and help everyone come together around a plan that clearly outlines responsibilities and priorities. We also agree on indicators that will help you recognize when the plan may need adjusting throughout the year.
